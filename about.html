What LotteryMigo is

LotteryMigo is an independent site for Powerball and Mega Millions statistics. It shows how often each number was drawn, which numbers have gone the longest without appearing, and the official draw list. A number generator and a practice draw drum are still there. They do not change the odds.

This is not gambling advice, and it does not guarantee any win. Lottery drawings are independent random events. Past frequencies and gaps do not make a future number more or less likely. Nothing on this site tells you to buy a ticket or promises a prize.

Official winning numbers are read from New York State Open Data at data.ny.gov: Powerball dataset d6yy-54nr and Mega Millions dataset 5xaw-6ayf. LotteryMigo is not affiliated with the New York Lottery, MUSL, or Mega Millions.

You must be 18 or older to use this site, and 21 or older where your local law requires it. If gambling is causing harm, call 1-800-GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537) or the National Problem Gambling Helpline at 1-800-522-4700.
