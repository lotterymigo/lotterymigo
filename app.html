How it works

A simple way to use it

The app is the phone version of this site. It is not in an app store yet, so the buttons on this page open the statistics here.

1

Choose a game

Switch between Powerball and Mega Millions, then look at the current drawings, the last 30, or the last 100. The counts describe what already happened. They do not forecast the next draw.

2

Save a line if you like

Favorite numbers stay on your phone. Saving a line does not buy a ticket and does not make those numbers more likely. You can read every count without signing in.

3

Turn alerts on only if you want them

The app remembers the dates already on the screen and tells you when a later date shows up. It does not watch a ticket for you.
