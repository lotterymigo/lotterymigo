FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is LotteryMigo an official lottery site?

No. LotteryMigo is an independent statistics and guide site. It is not part of MUSL, the Mega Millions Consortium, or any state lottery, and it does not sell tickets or pay prizes. Official results and claims go through the lottery that sold the ticket, and through powerball.com or megamillions.com.

Can this site tell me if my ticket won?

No. Compare the ticket with the drawing it was bought for, using the selling lottery’s results. The Powerball and Mega Millions results here are copied from NY Open Data and can lag the drawing by about a day. A photograph emailed to info@lotterymigo.com will not be validated. We never have the physical ticket.

Do hot or overdue numbers improve the odds?

They do not. Frequency and gaps describe drawings that already happened. The next Powerball white-ball draw still picks 5 numbers out of 69, and the next Mega Millions white-ball draw still picks 5 out of 70. A long gap leaves those chances where the prize chart put them. The hot, cold, and overdue guide uses a live example from this archive.

Why does the home page disagree with a number page?

The home page chart counts the full loaded file. That file mixes older Powerball and Mega Millions formats. A number page leads with the current matrix: Powerball since October 7, 2015, Mega Millions white balls since October 31, 2017, and the current Mega Ball since April 8, 2025. Both counts can be correct and still differ. The about page defines each one.

Why are white balls 60 through 69 lower on an all-time Powerball list?

In this archive they first appear on October 7, 2015, when the white-ball field became 1 through 69. Balls 1 through 59 also appear in the older rows, back to 2010. Extra years create extra hits. Inside the current matrix the two groups are in the same field, and the number pages compare them there.

Is Mega Ball 25 overdue?

It is retired from the current game. The Mega Ball field became 1 through 24 on April 8, 2025. The 25 page counts the previous 1–25 field and labels itself historical. A gap that runs past April 2025 is not a wait. The ball is not in the machine.

Why is the multiplier blank on recent Mega Millions drawings?

The NY Open Data rows for the $5 game do not include the built-in 2×, 3×, 4×, 5×, or 10× multiplier. The multiplier is assigned when the ticket is bought and is printed on the ticket. Where our file has no value, the results table shows a dash. Older rows sometimes include the retired Megaplier. The prize amounts for each multiplier are in the Mega Millions guide.

What are the current jackpot odds?

Powerball’s prize chart lists the jackpot at 1 in 292,201,338, and any prize at 1 in 24.87. Mega Millions lists the jackpot at 1 in 290,472,336. A March 2025 Mega Millions release put the chance of any prize at 1 in 23. The odds guide shows how the jackpot figures are just the size of each matrix. Those odds do not move when a number gets hot on this site.

How much does a ticket cost, and when are the drawings?

Powerball is $2 a play in most places, $3 in Idaho and Montana because Power Play is included there. Drawings are Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday at 10:59 p.m. Eastern, from the Florida Lottery draw studio in Tallahassee. Mega Millions is $5 a play with the multiplier included. Drawings are Tuesday and Friday at 11:00 p.m. Eastern, in Atlanta. Cutoff times for sales are earlier and depend on the state. See the Powerball and Mega Millions guides for prize tiers.

Does the number generator help?

It produces a line that follows the current field, including Mega Balls from 1 to 24. That line has the same chance as any other legal line. The odd-and-even option is a style of pick. It is not an advantage. The practice drum is a picture of balls moving. You can turn the motion off. It does not play sound, and it does not change a result.

How often is the archive updated?

An automated job refreshes the draw file at 12:00 UTC on Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday, after the usual draw nights, and then rebuilds these pages. If a drawing is missing, it has not been published in the NY dataset yet or the job has not run. Confirm a ticket against the official results anyway.

Lump sum or annuity, and what about taxes?

Both games advertise an annuity and a smaller cash amount, before taxes. Powerball describes 30 graduated payments over 29 years. Mega Millions describes one immediate payment and 29 annual payments, each 5 percent larger than the last. Federal rules treat lottery winnings as taxable gambling income, and regular withholding of 24 percent can apply when the proceeds are more than $5,000. The payout page cites the IRS pages and is not advice for your return.

Someone said I won and asked for a fee. What now?

Do not pay. A fee, a gift card, or a request for bank details is the pattern the FTC describes for prize scams. Claim a real ticket only through the lottery that sold it. Report the contact at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. The scam guide has the longer version. LotteryMigo will not ask you for a processing fee or a Social Security number.

Do I need an account?

No. The statistics, guides, and results are public. Google sign-in is only for people who want saved picks stored with their account. The privacy policy explains what is stored and how to delete it.

Why are there ads?

The website may show Google AdSense ads. The slots stay empty until the site owner adds slot IDs, so you may see no box at all. Ads do not change the draw list. The app builds of these pages omit the AdSense script. Cookie details are in the privacy policy.

Who do I contact?
