About LotteryMigo

What this site shows

LotteryMigo is a free look at Powerball and Mega Millions. Official winning numbers come from NY Open Data. The site counts how often each number appeared and how long it has been missing. The counts on this page use the full loaded archive, which mixes older matrices. The Powerball and Mega Millions number pages separate the current game, and the guides explain the rules. Past draws do not predict future results. This is not gambling advice, and it does not guarantee any win.
