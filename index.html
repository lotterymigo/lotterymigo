About LotteryMigo

Demystifying Lottery Probability Through Data

LotteryMigo is an entertainment-only view of Powerball and Mega Millions. Official winning numbers are loaded from NY Open Data and counted as frequencies and gaps. Past draws do not predict future results. This is not gambling advice, and it does not guarantee any win.

01. Empirical Frequency

Decennial Archive

We catalog drawing histories to trace how each ball behaves over long horizons, comparing actual draw counts against theoretical expectations.

02. Combinatorial Logic

Harmonic Distribution

Historically, sets with an extreme distribution (like all odd or all high numbers) account for less than 3% of winning draws. Our generator designs balanced sets.

03. Interactive Physics

True Random Simulation

Our HTML5 canvas blower drums simulate real-world air chambers, allowing users to watch numbers get picked organically without opaque black-box scripts.
