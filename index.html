About LotteryMigo

What this site shows

LotteryMigo is a free look at Powerball and Mega Millions. Official winning numbers come from NY Open Data. The site counts how often each number appeared and how long it has been missing. Past draws do not predict future results. This is not gambling advice, and it does not guarantee any win.

1. How often

Past drawings

Each white-ball number is counted across the loaded drawings. The count is history. It is not a forecast.

2. Number ideas

Mix of odd and even

One option builds a line with 3 odd numbers and 2 even numbers. That pattern is only a style of pick. It does not improve the odds.

3. The drum

A picture of a draw

The round drum shows balls moving. You can turn that motion off. The numbers still appear in the list. Nothing on this page plays sound.
