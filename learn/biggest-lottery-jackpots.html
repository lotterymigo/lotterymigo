Guide

The biggest lottery jackpots in history

This page lists the largest Powerball and Mega Millions jackpots those games have published: draw date, advertised annuity, cash option, where the ticket was sold, and how many tickets won. It is a history of prizes already drawn, for entertainment and reference. It is not a forecast and not a suggestion to buy a ticket. A past jackpot does not guarantee a win, and it does not change the odds on the next drawing. LotteryMigo does not sell tickets and cannot claim a prize or choose a payout.

The annuity is the headline. The cash option is the one-time payment. On the releases cited below, both are before taxes. When a row says the prize was split, the cash amount is the option for the whole prize. The lump sum, annuity, and taxes guide explains the difference, including what the IRS says in general about reporting lottery income. That page is not personal tax advice.

The ten largest jackpots

After an Arkansas ticket won on December 24, 2025, Powerball published the ten largest U.S. lottery jackpots by advertised annuity. Powerball's media center still ranks those six Powerball prizes as its top six, through the August 12, 2026 Illinois prize of $1.040 billion. Mega Millions' jackpot history still has the same four prizes as its largest annuities. The largest 2026 Mega Millions annuity on that page is $803 million, won in Florida on July 28. The annuity column is the order used here.

How a rollover works

The advertised jackpot is an estimate that moves with ticket sales. Powerball's site says the jackpot grows until it is won, by matching all five white balls and the red Powerball. If nobody matches, the unpaid prize stays in the pool, later sales are added, and the next estimate is higher. That carry is the rollover. These pages do not set a fixed increase for every missed drawing. Final sales can also close above the pre-draw estimate, which Powerball reported for the $2.04 billion, $1.817 billion, $1.787 billion, and $1.765 billion prizes, and for April 6, 2024, when an earlier $1.3 billion estimate finished at $1.326 billion.

A win starts the next jackpot over. From the April 8, 2025 drawing, Mega Millions restarts at $50 million. The March 25, 2025 announcement called that a change from a $20 million start. After the October 23, 2018 win, the next Mega Millions jackpot reset to $40 million, the starting value then. Powerball's December 25, 2025 and August 13, 2026 announcements each reset the next jackpot to $20 million. The August 13 release also put that Illinois prize at $1.040 billion, cash value $450.5 million, on the 44th drawing of the run, and called it the eighth-largest Powerball annuity. It sits outside this table because $1.040 billion is below rank 10. Current prices and matrices are on the Powerball and Mega Millions rules pages.

Why these jackpots got so large

The annuities reached these levels because long runs produced no jackpot winner while sales kept feeding the pool. Powerball printed the length of several runs. November 7, 2022 was drawing 41 of a run it described as three months. One California ticket matched white balls 10, 33, 41, 47, and 56, with red Powerball 10. The drawing was held at 8:57 a.m. Eastern on November 8 after a delay. The media center lists the cash option as $997,617,687.27 and names Edwin Castro, who took cash. That release describes this annuity as one immediate payment plus 29 annual payments rising 5 percent a year. The same step is in the October 11, 2023, September 6, 2025, December 24, 2025, and April 6, 2024 announcements. The August 12, 2026 announcement says 30 graduated payments over 29 years and does not restate a percentage.

December 24, 2025 was drawing 47, which Powerball called its longest jackpot cycle, on Powerball 19 with white balls 4, 25, 31, 52, and 59. It followed September 6, 2025, drawing 42 since May 31, 2025, on Powerball 17 with 11, 23, 44, 61, and 62. October 11, 2023 was drawing 36: one California ticket, 22, 24, 40, 52, 64, and Powerball 10, listed on the media center as Theodorus Struyck and a group. A group on one ticket is still one winning ticket. The Mega Millions history page names the three single tickets under the 2018 prize: Saltines Holdings, LLC, in Neptune Beach; LaKoma Island Investments, LLC, in Lebanon, Maine; and an anonymous partnership in Des Plaines.

The October 23, 2018 Mega Millions jackpot had rolled since July 24. The next day's release called it a 26-drawing run from July 27, with white balls 5, 28, 62, 65, and 70, plus Mega Ball 5. The Powerball and Mega Millions number pages count those balls in this archive only. Powerball's releases for these drawings put jackpot odds at about 1 in 292.2 million. On March 25, 2025 Mega Millions said its jackpot odds would move from 1 in 302,575,350 to 1 in 290,472,336 as the Mega Ball field went from 25 numbers to 24. The odds guide shows why another ticket scales a very small chance and leaves it very small. A longer rollover adds drawings. It leaves each play at the odds of its own matrix.

What a record does not change

The cash option is one payment. The annuity is a schedule of payments. Taxes come off either one, and the selling lottery sets the claim deadline. This site will not estimate a tax bill or recommend a payout. A bigger headline adds no winning combinations. These records are for entertainment, and they do not guarantee a win.

Claim a matching ticket from the lottery that sold it. Powerball results and Mega Millions results here are a reading of the public draw file. A request for a fee, a gift card, or bank details is the pattern in the scam guide. LotteryMigo will not ask for any of that.
