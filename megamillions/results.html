Recent Mega Millions results

These are the 100 most recent Mega Millions drawings in the NY Open Data file LotteryMigo copies, from November 11, 2025 through October 23, 2026. The full file currently holds 2,551 drawings, from May 17, 2002 through October 23, 2026. Every number links to its statistics page.

From April 8, 2025 onward, this NY Open Data file does not include the built-in Mega Millions multiplier. A dash in those rows means the value is absent from the file, not that the ticket multiplier was 1. Older rows may show the retired Megaplier when the dataset recorded it. Prize amounts for each multiplier are in the Mega Millions guide.

The list is rebuilt when the archive job runs, at 12:00 UTC on Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday. A drawing can be missing here for a day after it happens. Check a ticket on the lottery that sold it, or on the official Mega Millions site. LotteryMigo does not validate tickets and does not guarantee a win.
