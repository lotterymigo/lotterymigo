Powerball number statistics

Powerball number pages on LotteryMigo are counts from the NY Open Data archive, split so the current matrix is not averaged together with retired ones. The white-ball table below uses 1,424 drawings from October 7, 2015 through October 24, 2026. The even-split column is the count each number would have if those drawings had been shared perfectly evenly: 1,424 drawings times 5, divided by 69.

The gap column is how many drawings have passed since the number last appeared in that same set. A gap of zero means the number was in the latest drawing. A long gap is a fact about the list. It does not raise the chance that the number is drawn next. White balls are still 5 out of 69 on the next drawing. Read the hot, cold, and overdue guide before treating a rank as a tip, and the rules guide for the prize chart behind the odds.

The five longest current white-ball gaps in this set are white ball 43 (46 drawings since July 8, 2026), white ball 60 (34 drawings since August 5, 2026), white ball 66 (31 drawings since August 12, 2026), white ball 27 (30 drawings since August 15, 2026), white ball 63 (30 drawings since August 15, 2026). They are listed so you can open the draw dates, not so you can treat them as due.
