Where these numbers come from

Source line appears after the archive loads.

Powerball’s current field starts October 7, 2015: five white balls from 1 through 69 and a Powerball from 1 through 26. Mega Millions white balls start October 31, 2017, from 1 through 70. The Mega Ball field of 1 through 24 starts April 8, 2025. Mega Ball 25 is retired and is not counted here; its historical page remains on the live site.

Last 30 and Last 100 are the newest drawings inside that pool, not calendar days. White balls and the bonus ball keep separate date ranges when the rules changed on different days. Frequency is the number of drawings in the window that included the ball. Gap is how many drawings in the window have happened since that appearance. A ball that has not appeared is missing for the whole window.

The published matrix rules are on How counts work. Saved picks and the CSV download stay on the account and live chart. This page is an experiment and is not linked from the live navigation.
