Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

LotteryMigo does not ask you to create an account. Number tools, the draw simulator, and the scratch game run in your browser. The draw archive is a public JSON file of winning numbers from NY Open Data. We do not sell personal information.

What is stored on your device

If you dismiss the notice at the top of the home page, the site saves that choice in your browser’s local storage under the key lotterymigo_alert_dismissed. Text size is saved as lotterymigo_font_px. High contrast is saved as lotterymigo_contrast. Ball motion is saved as lotterymigo_motion. Favorite numbers are saved as lotterymigo_favorites. In the Android or iOS app, the draw-alert choice is saved as lotterymigo_draw_alerts and the last alert date as lotterymigo_last_notified. Those values stay on your device. Clearing site data removes them.

Advertising and cookies

The website does not show ads today. The Android and iOS app project includes Google AdMob scaffolding that uses Google’s public test ad units only. A test ad does not mean a production ad account is connected. Before any ad request, the app asks Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. If consent does not allow ads, no banner is shown. Banners are hidden while a scratch-card result is on screen so they do not cover Try again.

If real AdMob units are turned on later, Google may set or read an advertising identifier and similar technologies to measure and choose ads. Those technologies are not required to use the frequency tools, generator, simulator, or free scratch game. This page must be updated before real ad units ship. You can block or delete cookies in a browser. Blocking them should not stop the draw archive or the free games from loading.

App notifications

The website does not send notifications. The Android and iOS app can ask for permission to show a local notification when a newer drawing date is in the list. That notice stays on the device. It is not a prediction and it is not a remote push message. Alerts stay off until you turn them on.

Logs

The host that serves the site may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested, for security and operations. Those logs are not used to build a lottery-player profile.
