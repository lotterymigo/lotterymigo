Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

LotteryMigo does not ask you to create an account. Number tools, the draw simulator, and the scratch game run in your browser. The draw archive is a public JSON file of winning numbers from NY Open Data. We do not sell personal information.

What is stored on your device

If you dismiss the notice at the top of the home page, the site saves that choice in your browser’s local storage under the key lotterymigo_alert_dismissed. Text size is saved as lotterymigo_font_px. High contrast is saved as lotterymigo_contrast. Ball motion is saved as lotterymigo_motion. Those values stay on your device. Clearing site data removes them.

Advertising and cookies

The site may later show ads from an advertising network. If that happens, the network may set or read cookies, mobile identifiers, or similar technologies to measure views, limit how often an ad appears, and help decide which ad to show. Those technologies are not required to use the frequency tools, generator, simulator, or free scratch game.

We do not name a specific ad network here, because none is connected yet. If ads are added, this page will be updated. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them should not stop the draw archive or the free games from loading.

Logs

The host that serves the site may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested, for security and operations. Those logs are not used to build a lottery-player profile.
